**PROMPT:**

Create a vertical sci-fi poster showing a lonely future city where artificial intelligence has taken over most human jobs. The main subject is a single unemployed human standing alone near the center of a wide, empty city street. Surround the person with enormous futuristic office buildings, automated factories, robotic stores, and AI-controlled machines. Place working humanoid robots inside the buildings and along the sides of the street so that humans appear to be the only person who no longer has a role in this technological society.

Use sharp geometric lines on the skyscrapers, windows, robots, and machines to create a cold, organized, and controlled environment. Add strong directional lines through the edges of the street, rows of buildings, glowing lights, and pathways. Arrange these lines so that they point toward the lone human and guide the viewer’s eyes from the outer areas of the poster toward the central figure.

Use large rectangles, squares, and angular geometric shapes for the buildings, windows, digital screens, and machines. These large shapes should dominate the positive space on both sides of the composition. Leave a large amount of negative space around humans by keeping the street mostly empty and allowing a dark, open sky to appear above the figure. This arrangement should make the human look small, vulnerable, and isolated compared with the massive AI-controlled city.

Give the robots, machines, and buildings sleek metallic panels, polished steel surfaces, reflective glass, and smooth technological textures. In contrast, give the human rough, worn clothing with visible fabric texture. Make the skyscrapers and industrial machines appear massive, heavy, and three-dimensional, with strong physical volume and solid forms. Include some open-volume structures, such as exposed robotic frames, hollow metal supports, cables, and visible mechanical parts, to add layers and depth to the environment.

Use strong value and color contrast to create one clear focal point. Keep most of the futuristic city in cold blue and cyan light with metallic gray surfaces and deep, dark shadows. Place a warm amber or golden light directly on the lone human. The bright, warm figure against the darker, cold-colored city should immediately attract the viewer’s attention. The final composition should communicate loneliness, human displacement, and the overwhelming power of technology in a future where AI continues working while humans are left behind.

**THE STUDIO GLOSSARY**

**Line:** Line helps show where things are and can lead your eyes to an important part of the artwork. They can be straight, curved, think, thin, or angled.

**Shape:** Shape is the form of something we see, like a square, circle, rectangle, or an uneven shape. Shapes help build the objects in an artwork.

**Texture:** Texture is how something looks like it would feel if we touched it. For example, something can look smooth, rough, soft, shiny, or bumpy.

**Volume:** Volume is what makes an object look like it takes up real space instead of looking flat. It can make something look big, heavy, or three dimensional.

**Contrast:** Contrast is when two things look very different from each other, like light and dark or warm and cool colors. It helps certain parts of the artwork stand out.
