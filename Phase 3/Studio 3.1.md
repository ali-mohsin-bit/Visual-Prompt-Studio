**Visual Prompt 1**

**Historical Theme:** Cathedrals as spiritual hubs for travelers and pilgrims

**Historical Period:** Byzantine (6th century CE)

**Artwork Used as Inspiration:** Hagia Sophia, Constantinople (present-day Istanbul, Turkey)

**Medium:** Religious architecture

Create an image of Hagia Sophia during the Byzantine period in the 6th century, when it was built as a Christian church in Constantinople. Show its massive central dome, smaller surrounding domes, rounded arches, marble columns, and beautiful golden mosaics. Use warm gold, cream, and earthy colors to represent the richness of Byzantine architecture.

Show Christian worshippers and travelers entering the church to pray and admire its beauty. The building should have a balanced design, with the large central dome as the main focal point. Use light, space, symmetry, and detailed textures to highlight its impressive architecture.

Hagia Sophia was built during the reign of Emperor Justinian I and represented the religious and political importance of the Byzantine Empire. It served as a major center of Christian worship and welcomed visitors and travelers to Constantinople. Its grand architecture and religious decorations reflected the power of the empire and the importance of Christianity in Byzantine society. The image should show how Hagia Sophia connected religion, architecture, and imperial power while providing a spiritual place for worshippers and travelers.

**Visual Prompt 2**

**Historical Theme:** Art as divine power

**Historical Period:** Ancient Egypt – Old Kingdom (c. 2600–2500 BCE)

**Artwork Used as Inspiration:** Pyramids at Giza

**Medium:** Monumental stone architecture and funerary structures

Create an image of the Great Pyramids of Giza during the Old Kingdom of Ancient Egypt around 2500 BCE. Show three massive pyramids standing in the desert under a bright blue sky. The pyramids should have smooth limestone surfaces that reflect sunlight, making them look grand and powerful. Include the Great Sphinx, ancient Egyptian workers, priests, and people wearing traditional linen clothing. Use warm golden, sandy brown, and light blue colors to create a realistic ancient Egyptian atmosphere.

The pyramids should have a balanced composition, with strong triangular shapes, straight lines, and a large scale to make them the main focal point. Use natural lighting, stone textures, and open desert space to emphasize their impressive size and architectural precision.

During Ancient Egypt's Old Kingdom, pyramids were built as royal tombs for pharaohs, who were believed to have a divine role in Egyptian society. The Egyptians believed in life after death, and the pyramids were designed to protect the pharaoh's body and support his journey into the afterlife. These enormous structures also represented the pharaoh's religious authority, wealth, and power. This image should demonstrate how architecture was used to express divine kingship, religious beliefs, and the importance of the afterlife in Ancient Egyptian culture.
