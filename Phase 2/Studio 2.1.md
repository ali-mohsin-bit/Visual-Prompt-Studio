**Prompt A**

Create a watercolor painting of a couple sitting together by a peaceful lake, enjoying the sunset. Include mountains in the background, a large tree on the right, and beautiful reflections in the water. Focus on color, line, texture, contrast, and balance to create a calm and romantic scene. Use watercolor paints, soft brushes, watercolor paper, and clean water. Apply wet-on-wet blending for smooth sunset colors, light layers for depth, and gentle brushstrokes for reflection. The painting should have soft colors, natural textures, and a peaceful feeling.

**Prompt B**

Create a linocut print of the same couple sitting together by the lake and enjoying the sunset. Keep the same mountains, large tree on the right, and reflections in the water. Maintain the same composition, color, line, texture, contrast, and balance as the watercolor painting. Use a linoleum block, carving gouges, a brayer, printing ink, and printmaking paper. Use bold outlines, simple shapes, and carved patterns to show the couple, sunset, and water reflections. The artwork should have strong contrasts, clear edges, and a bold appearance while keeping the peaceful and romantic feeling of the original scene.
